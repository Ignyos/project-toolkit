# Optional Per-Item Evidence Policy

Use this module only when explicitly selected during bootstrap. It adds persistent verification
notes for each work item; it is not a requirement of Solo or Team mode.

For each completed item, record only:

- The acceptance criterion or criteria addressed.
- The validation performed and its result.
- Any known limitation or unverified criterion.

Keep the record concise, factual, and tied to the item's existing scope. Do not turn optional
evidence into a reason to add new tests, expand acceptance criteria, or require an extra approval
after an authorized human has accepted the work.
